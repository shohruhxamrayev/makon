Your craft,
in thousands of homes.

People use Makon to design their rooms. Soon they will order the furniture and decor straight from local masters — carpenters, upholsterers, embroiderers, ceramicists and metalworkers. We're starting with a small group of workshops in Samarkand.

Why join early Nega hozirdan qo‘shilish kerak

01

Seen inside real rooms

Buyers place your piece in their own room in 3D and see that it fits — before they ever call you.

Xaridor buyumingizni o‘z xonasida 3D’da ko‘radi.

02

Orders with exact sizes

Every request comes with the room's measurements and a picture of where the piece will stand. Fewer mistakes, fewer returns.

Har bir buyurtma aniq o‘lchamlar bilan keladi.

03

Your name on your work

A page for your workshop: your city, your story, your photos. Buyers choose a master, not a factory.

Ustaxonangiz nomi va hikoyasi bilan.

04

Free during the pilot

Joining the first group costs nothing. We photograph and model a few of your pieces with you.

Sinov davrida qo‘shilish bepul.
