Questions

Good to know

Does Makon change my walls or windows?

No. You choose what to keep — window plane, openings, floor, ceiling light, built-ins — and those are locked in every render and in the brief.

How accurate are the measurements?

Until you calibrate, lengths are estimates. Measure one wall or window with a tape, enter it once, and the whole plan scales to match. Quantities are only marked final after calibration.

What is the difference between an AI render and a mood preview?

An AI render redraws the furniture and finishes in your photo. A mood preview runs instantly on your device and re-grades colour and light to the chosen style, so you can compare directions before spending a render.

Where are my projects stored?

In your browser, on this device. You can export any project as a file and import it on another device.

Are furniture prices real?

Makon never invents prices or stock. "Shop similar" opens live searches in real stores, and you type the price of what you pick — the budget adds it up.
