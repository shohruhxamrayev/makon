    How to draw
    • Wall: click to place corners, click the first corner to close, double-click or Enter to stop.
    • Door / window: click on a wall. Drag it along the wall later.
    • Move: drag furniture, walls or corner dots. Hold Alt to turn off snapping.
    • Navigate: scroll or pinch to zoom, drag empty space (or two fingers) to pan.
